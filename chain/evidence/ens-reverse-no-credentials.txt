--- an address advertising a name it cannot back ---

primaryName(0x9703d9cF2F834e71D9b70675e746F7B634c9D1e9)
  -> {"kind":"no-forward-resolver","name":"ariutokintumi.eth","coinType":"60"}

  This address has a reverse record. The name in it has no resolver on Sepolia, so
  nothing points back. The Universal Resolver reverts `ResolverNotFound(bytes)`
  (selector 0x77209fe8) carrying the DNS-encoded name; we decode the selector rather
  than the provider's message text, which is "execution reverted" for this, for a
  gateway outage, and for a resolver that is not a contract alike. Reported as a
  named refusal, never as a verified identity.
